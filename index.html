Research

What I Study

I investigate how people interpret and respond to AI systems and AI-generated content. My work sits at the intersection of communication, technology, and society, using experimental, survey, psychophysiological, computational, and qualitative methods.

AI Trust & Persuasion

How explainability and emotional support shape trust in AI health advisors and persuasive technologies.

AI in Health & Strategic Communication

Examining message acceptance, behavioral intentions, and effectiveness of AI-delivered health and advertising messages.

Algorithmic Bias & Digital Inequalities

Investigating disparities in AI adoption, skills, and outcomes across gender, age, and demographic groups.
