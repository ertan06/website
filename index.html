About

At the intersection of
communication & AI

My research examines how people interpret and respond to AI systems and AI-generated content, with particular attention to trust, persuasion, message acceptance, and behavioral intentions in strategic and health communication. I am especially interested in the design cues that make AI communication more persuasive and trustworthy, including agency, transparency, and socioemotional cues. I also investigate AI's broader impacts on society, including digital divide and algorithmic bias.

I bring broad methodological experience across experiments, surveys, qualitative methods, biometric tools, computational methods, and social media analytics, applied in both academic research and client-facing projects.
