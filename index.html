Communication researcher exploring how people perceive, trust, and are persuaded by artificial intelligence in health and strategic communication. Driven by the conviction that AI should serve the public good, I study who benefits from these technologies, who gets left behind, and what we can do about it.

Research Focus

What I Study

Using experimental, psychophysiological, computational, and qualitative methods to understand AI's role in communication and society.

AI Trust & Persuasion

How explainability and emotional support shape trust in AI health advisors and persuasive technologies.

Health & Strategic Communication

Examining message acceptance, behavioral intentions, and the effectiveness of AI-delivered health and advertising messages.

Algorithmic Bias & Digital Inequalities

Investigating who gets left behind: disparities in AI adoption, skills, and outcomes across demographics.
