About

At the intersection of
communication & AI

My research examines how people interpret and respond to AI systems and AI-generated content, with particular attention to trust, persuasion, message acceptance, and behavioral intentions in strategic and health communication.

I also investigate AI's broader societal consequences, including algorithmic bias and inequalities in AI adoption and skills across demographic groups, using experimental, survey, psychophysiological, computational, and qualitative methods.
