Research Interests

My research examines how people interpret and respond to AI systems and AI-generated content, with particular attention to trust, persuasion, message acceptance, and behavioral intentions in strategic and health communication. I also investigate AI's broader societal consequences, including algorithmic bias and inequalities in AI adoption and skills across demographic groups.
