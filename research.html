Technologies
Should Benefit
Everybody

Technology should serve everyone, and that begins with understanding it. I study how humans communicate with artificial intelligence and how to harness it for positive behavior change, without losing sight of who it empowers and who it leaves behind.

Research Areas

Focus Area

AI Trust & Persuasion

How explainability and emotional support shape trust in AI health advisors and persuasive technologies. My dissertation investigates building trust in AI health advisors through explainability and emotional support under Dr. Linwan Wu.

Focus Area

Health & Strategic Communication

Examining message acceptance, behavioral intentions, and the effectiveness of AI-delivered health promotion and advertising messages. How conversational AI agents can promote health behaviors.

Focus Area

Algorithmic Bias & Digital Inequalities

Investigating who gets left behind: disparities in AI adoption, algorithmic skills, and outcomes across gender, age, and demographic groups.
