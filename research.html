Research

Research & Labs

I investigate how people interpret and respond to AI systems and AI-generated content, using experimental, psychophysiological, computational, and qualitative methods.

Research Areas

AI Trust & Persuasion

How explainability and emotional support shape trust in AI health advisors and persuasive technologies. My dissertation, "Building Trust in AI Health Advisors: The Role of Explainability and Emotional Support," investigates these dynamics under the supervision of Dr. Linwan Wu.

Health & Strategic Communication

Examining message acceptance, behavioral intentions, and the effectiveness of AI-delivered health promotion and advertising messages across populations. I study how conversational AI agents can promote health behaviors and how language formality affects adherence.

Algorithmic Bias & Digital Inequalities

Investigating who gets left behind: disparities in AI adoption, algorithmic skills, and outcomes across gender, age, and demographic groups. My work examines how gender shapes attitudes and skills toward generative AI and traces generational digital inequalities in the age of algorithms.

Methods

Methodological Toolkit

Experimental & Survey

Designing and conducting controlled experiments and surveys to test causal mechanisms in AI perception, trust, and persuasion.

Psychophysiological

Eye tracking, GSR, EEG, and EMG via iMotions to capture real-time cognitive and emotional responses to media and AI systems.

Computational & Qualitative

Critical discourse analysis, in-depth interviews, and computational approaches including Python-based analysis and LLM fine-tuning.
